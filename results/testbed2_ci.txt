Protocol: 15 repeated stratified 70/30 splits; 1200 benign + 1200 feasible-FDI states each;
detector = Random Forest (150 trees) on limited-observability metered branches (16/20).

Clean feasible-FDI detection macro-F1 = 0.951  95% CI [0.947, 0.954]  (n=15 splits)
  per-split range: 0.935 - 0.960

Interpretation: the single-split number in the manuscript sits inside this interval;
the feasible-FDI detector's clean performance is stable across resampling. The evasion
recall collapse under the ES attack (reported separately in exp3) is the security finding,
not sampling noise.
